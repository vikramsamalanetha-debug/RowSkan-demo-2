Your inventory stays on your devices.

Early-access privacy notice · November 3, 2026

RowSkan is a vehicle inventory product of LIT America. This notice describes the current local-storage edition. Device sync and paid cloud storage are not active.

Inventory and photographs

The browser app saves vehicle records, photographs and unfinished entries in this browser profile. The native app uses a local SQLite database and photograph files in its application storage. We do not receive your inventory or photos through a cloud inventory service in this edition.

VIN lookup and camera access

If you choose Decode VIN, the entered VIN is sent to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration vehicle API. That request also exposes ordinary network metadata to the service. Manual vehicle entry works without VIN lookup. The camera is accessed only when you choose a capture or scan feature and grant the device or browser permission. Photographs are processed locally.

Vehicle locations

If you choose Save current GPS or update a vehicle location, RowSkan saves the coordinates, reported accuracy and capture time in that local vehicle record and its backups. The walking guide uses your current location only while its foreground screen is active; it does not save a movement history. You can enter or adjust a vehicle pin manually. The north-up yard diagram does not contact a map provider. If you explicitly open Google Maps, the vehicle destination coordinates are sent to Google under its policies. Device location services may themselves use your operating system’s location providers.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages delivers the website files and may process ordinary website-access information under GitHub's policies. The website does not include analytics or advertising trackers. This does not prevent hosting providers or operating systems from maintaining their normal access or diagnostic information.

Backups and deletion

New backup exports ask for a password and encrypt the exported inventory. You choose where to save or share that file. We cannot recover a lost backup password. Legacy unencrypted backups can still be imported. Browser and native backups use separate formats. Removing site data, uninstalling the app or erasing the device can remove locally stored records. Native vehicle deletion is recoverable in the app; photos associated with deleted records remain locally retained in this candidate.

Early-access limits

This edition has no cloud account, billing, remote recovery, device enrollment or automatic sync service. Local databases are protected by the operating system and are not separately encrypted by RowSkan. Use a protected device and keep verified backups. GitHub Pages shares its website origin with other projects under this GitHub account; a dedicated application domain is required before commercial use with sensitive inventory.
